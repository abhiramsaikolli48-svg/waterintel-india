Groundwater Intelligence: Key Scientific Distinctions (CGWB)

Module 7
Depth Below Ground Level (m bgl) ≠ Water Column A reading of 15 metres below ground level describes the depth to the water table at that monitoring well—it does not mean 15 metres of water is available for extraction.
Periodic vs. Telemetry Monitoring CGWB measures most observation wells periodically (pre- and post-monsoon), while select DWLR telemetry stations transmit hourly readings. Interfaces must never label periodic readings as live data.

Maintenance: Pre-monsoon gutter & filter cleaning

Recharge Pits & Trenches

Supports shallow aquifer recharge in permeable soils with declining water tables.

Verification: Soil permeability & contamination check
Maintenance: Annual desilting of filter media
